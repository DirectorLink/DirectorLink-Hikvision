Setup

Several cameras? Use the DirectorLink · Hikvision driver (the Hikvision Hub): it finds all cameras, adds them with this driver and configures them with one login. Nothing needs to be entered here.

Adding a single camera by hand:

  1. Add this driver to a room.
  2. On the camera's Properties page (Control4's camera page) enter the Address, Username and Password, and the ports if they are not 80 and 554. The driver sets Digest authentication and corrects the RTSP port for you. For a camera behind an NVR, enter the NVR's address and set Channel in Advanced Properties.
  3. Check Status in the driver properties: Online - events live - alerts on.

On the camera: tick Notify Surveillance Center for each detection that should reach Control4. Control4 plays H.264 only: cameras added by the hub switch their sub stream to H.264 by themselves; otherwise run Actions > Set Sub Stream To H.264 (the main stream stays as it is, for recording). The Attention line tells you when one of these is missing.

Camera off or removed? Set Camera Enabled to No: the driver stops all requests, events and offline alarms, and the hub stops counting it.

Detections and alerts

Detections (Motion, Person, Vehicle, Line Crossing, Intrusion...) always fire their events, variables and contacts. Use them for automations such as turning on lights.

Alerts are the subset meant for people: the Alert event, notifications, snapshots and History. An alert fires when a detection happens, alerts are on (this camera and the Hikvision Hub), not snoozed, and the detection matches Alert On:

Alert OnAlerts for
Any detectioneverything the camera reports
People and vehiclespeople and vehicles (AcuSense cameras), tamper and alarm inputs
People onlypeople (AcuSense cameras), tamper and alarm inputs

On the touchscreen

Inside the camera view, the controls (Extras) offer:

SectionControls
AlertsAlerts from this camera on/off · Alert on (any / people and vehicles / people only) · Snooze for 1 hour
CameraMotion detection on/off · Day / night · Light (Smart, IR, White light, Off; on cameras that have one)
MaintenanceReboot the camera

Video picks the H.264 stream that fits each screen; Control4 cannot play H.265. Snapshots work with any codec and, when the camera can scale its pictures, come at the size each screen asks for. Setup Report shows the measured sizes.

Programming reference

Events

Alert, Motion Detected, Motion Ended, Person Detected, Vehicle Detected, Line Crossing, Intrusion Detected, Region Entrance, Region Exiting, Tamper Detected, Scene Change Detected, Face Detected, Object Left Behind, Object Removed, Alarm Input Active, Alarm Input Inactive, PIR Alarm, Camera Online, Camera Offline, Alerts On, Alerts Off.

Variables

ONLINE, ALERTS_ENABLED, ALERT_ACTIVE, MOTION, PERSON, VEHICLE, LINE_CROSSING, INTRUSION, TAMPER, ALARM_INPUT, MOTION_DETECTION_ENABLED (BOOL); LAST_DETECTION, LAST_ALERT, LAST_ALERT_TIME, DIRECTORLINK_CAMERA (always 1), DIRECTORLINK_CAMERA_KIND (camera), DIRECTORLINK_CAMERA_EVENTS (Alert=1, the id of the Alert event) (STRING). The last three let the DirectorLink app (1.10 and later) recognize the camera and find its alerts.

Conditionals

Camera is Online/Offline · Alerts are On/Off · Alert is Active/Clear · Motion is Active/Idle · Active detection is/is not <type> · Last alert is/is not <type>.

Commands

SET_ALERTS, SNOOZE_ALERTS, SET_ALERT_ON, SET_MOTION_DETECTION, SET_DAY_NIGHT, SET_LIGHT_MODE, SET_IMAGE (brightness, contrast, saturation, sharpness), SET_ALARM_OUTPUT, GOTO_PRESET, REBOOT_CAMERA.

Contacts (Connections > Control)

Alert, Motion, Person, Vehicle, Line Crossing, Intrusion, Tamper, Alarm Input. Bind them to Control4 Motion Sensor or Contact Sensor devices.

Actions

Reconnect · Test Snapshot (tile, notification and full-screen pictures with their size, and whether the camera can scale pictures) · Set Sub Stream To H.264 (Control4 cannot play H.265; the main stream is left alone) · Setup Report (everything on one page, in the Lua tab) · Reboot Camera.
